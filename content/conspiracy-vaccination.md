Some thoughts to run through when reality is in doubt.

# Extraordinary claims require extraordinary evidence.
The person claiming something is required to back it up with evidence. It is on their side to prove it.

# Don't look for conspiracies where things can be explained by incompetence
This is especially true for huge organisations (states included).

# Is it possible? Yes. Is it likely? No.
Another way to say what Occam's razor says, tbh. An obscure situation may occur, of course. But then it is extraordinary (look above).

# Look for the usual haters being quiet - the Soviets never questioned the Moon landing
This is the worst tip of all, but sometimes it may be handy too - if you are into a theory, also look at what others say, especially the usual suspects who would hate it but are surprisingly quiet. Of course, they are part of that conspiracy too! /s
